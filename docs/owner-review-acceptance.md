# Owner-only internal review — acceptance contract

Scope: browser review of an existing **immutable `invoice_drafts` snapshot**, not a revisioned working workspace. A working workspace is not automatically converted into an immutable invoice draft. OWNER must log in through the existing MFA browser login. Routes are private, isolated and not published to staging.

- GET `/internal/review?lang=fr|en`: show at most 20 most recent immutable drafts, owner-only, with UUID navigation only; no customer data in URLs. A valid owner session and configured tenant are required even when the list is empty.
- GET `/internal/review/<uuid>?lang=fr|en`: show the exact persisted draft recipient email/name, dates, lines, supplied tax rates, total in CAD and **internal-only** disclaimer, escaping all content. The review form requires separate affirmative acknowledgement of recipient, amount, dates and tax applicability/rates. No automatic decision or hidden prechecked checkbox.
- POST same URL: session cookie only, owner role, matching configured HTTPS Origin/Host and same-origin fetch metadata, bounded URL-encoded body with exact one-of-each fields, session-and-draft-bound HMAC CSRF token and explicit `APPROVE_DRAFT_ONLY` marker. The existing transactional approval service rechecks the owner session, immutable snapshot total and email and writes at most one internal approval. Revoked, STAFF, foreign business, wrong recipient/total, duplicate parameters, wrong origin and malformed token must fail closed. Same owner repeating the same decision is idempotent; another owner cannot replace it.
- No issued invoice, Wave write, mail, payment, customer portal, live customer data or deployment is involved. Owner enrollment and recovery, real staging TLS/proxy/IP controls, independent review and accounting/tax validation remain mandatory before any live operation.
